## 🚀 Featured Personal Projects

### 🗺️ [klopi.gr](https://klopi.gr) (PRIVATE repo)
A crowdsourced map where people in Greece report burglaries, thefts, vehicle thefts, vandalism and suspicious activity in their neighbourhood, with live connection with ΕΛ.ΑΣ official notifications. It has a heatmap, time filters (24h / 7d / 30d), area pages, a live feed of recent reports and user accounts. A community reward system ("Rouf coin") is currently in demo.

`Next.js` · `PWA` · `Maps`

### 📚 [aeppath.gr](https://aeppath.gr) (PRIVATE repo)
A study platform for Γ' Λυκείου (3rd grade of High School) students preparing for the ΑΕΠΠ Panhellenic exam. It has flashcards and a quiz for every chapter, a compact live PDF of the full syllabus, solved and unsolved exercises, past exam papers, a Pomodoro study timer, a career orientation quiz and notes for registered(my) students.

`Next.js` · `EdTech`

### 🏺 [Amphoreus: Crypto Cold Wallet, on your USB](https://github.com/skk137/AmphoreusCryptoColdWallet)
A secure multi-chain cold wallet, that secures your keys in a usb stick u dont use anymnore. Your keys, your coins. It runs as a desktop app, not a website, with tested builds for Windows 10/11, macOS and Linux. Experiencing vibe code, in depth, for first time! 

`Rust` · `Tauri` · `React` · `TypeScript`

### 💬 [askfm.around](https://askfm-revanced.vercel.app)
A Greek-language redesign of ask.fm. Users ask and answer questions (anonymous ones too, with limits). It adds 🔥 / 🧊 reactions, a HotMeter with leaderboards, streaks, a "nearby" feed, hashtag search and trending topics.

`Next.js` · `Vercel`

### 🌱 Smart Greenhouse *(🚧DEMO in design at Peania/Kantza)*
IoT automation for a home greenhouse: sensor monitoring, automated watering zones, plant profiles and frost alerts based on weather data. It also includes a web app where each user pairs their own greenhouse device to their account.

`IoT` · `Sensors` · `Web app`
